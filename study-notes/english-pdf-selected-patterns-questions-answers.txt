ENGLISH PDF — SELECTED GRAMMAR PATTERNS
QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

Source: eng.pdf
Scope: The 20 selected grammar patterns studied from the PDF.

============================================================
1. PRESENT PERFECT TENSE (has, have + V3)
============================================================

1. This book (just publish) for this month.
Answer: This book has just been published for this month.

2. When my friend comes, I (already have) my lunch.
Answer: When my friend comes, I will already have had my lunch.
Note: This sentence naturally uses the Future Perfect, although it appears under the PDF's Present Perfect section.

3. Yu Yu (not visit) her relatives for four months.
Answer: Yu Yu has not visited her relatives for four months.

4. You (ever see) a lion walking on a rope?
Answer: Have you ever seen a lion walking on a rope?

5. Ko Ko (not already tell) about his girlfriend.
Answer: Ko Ko has not already told us about his girlfriend.

6. The bridge (build) by engineers recently.
Answer: The bridge has been built by engineers recently.

7. A new well (dig) for the villages.
Answer: A new well has been dug for the villages.

8. Nobody (write) to me for many weeks.
Answer: Nobody has written to me for many weeks.

============================================================
2. PAST PERFECT TENSE (had + V3)
============================================================

1. After he (do) his homework, he went for a walk.
Answer: After he had done his homework, he went for a walk.

2. They told him that they (not meet) him before.
Answer: They told him that they had not met him before.

3. The compound (clean) before the building was constructed.
Answer: The compound had been cleaned before the building was constructed.

4. After the girl (sing) the classical song, she went down the stage.
Answer: After the girl had sung the classical song, she went down the stage.

5. When I found my lost wallet, everything in it (take out).
Answer: When I found my lost wallet, everything in it had been taken out.

============================================================
3. PRESENT CONTINUOUS TENSE (am, is, are + V-ing)
============================================================

1. Most countries (become) aware of the need to conserve and manage their resources.
Answer: Most countries are becoming aware of the need to conserve and manage their resources.

2. The cows (drive) into an enclosure at the moment.
Answer: The cows are being driven into an enclosure at the moment.

3. The troop (signal) for the attack to begin.
Answer: The troop is signaling for the attack to begin.

4. The dance routine (teach) by the new dance instructor?
Answer: Is the dance routine being taught by the new dance instructor?

5. Where you (go) with all those books?
Answer: Where are you going with all those books?

6. Can I use your camera or you (use) it at the moment?
Answer: Can I use your camera, or are you using it at the moment?

7. The troop (signal) for the attack to begin.
Answer: The troop is signaling for the attack to begin.

8. They (sit) at a coffee table when the stranger entered?
Answer: Were they sitting at a coffee table when the stranger entered?
Note: The PDF places this under Present Continuous, but “entered” requires Past Continuous.

============================================================
4. PAST CONTINUOUS TENSE (was, were + V-ing)
============================================================

1. When we (watch) TV, the light went out.
Answer: When we were watching TV, the light went out.

2. He (interview) by the professors when he fainted.
Answer: He was being interviewed by the professors when he fainted.

3. When the young actress left the party, everybody (talk) about her.
Answer: When the young actress left the party, everybody was talking about her.

4. My television (not work) properly these days.
Answer: My television was not working properly these days.

5. The roof of the kitchen (mend) when it started raining.
Answer: The roof of the kitchen was being mended when it started raining.

6. Emily (lie) in the sunlight to get her skin tanned.
Answer: Emily was lying in the sunlight to get her skin tanned.

7. One of the pages of this book (miss).
Answer: One of the pages of this book was missing.

============================================================
5. IT IS / IT WAS
============================================================

1. Our neighbors don’t like the pungent smell of jengkal.
Answer: It is the pungent smell of jengkal that our neighbors don’t like.

2. Soe Soe won the first prize in the essay competition.
Answer: It was Soe Soe who won the first prize in the essay competition.

3. Thousands of students created spectacular visuals during the parade.
Answer: It was spectacular visuals that thousands of students created during the parade.

4. The trip to Thailand with my family makes me happy.
Answer: It is the trip to Thailand with my family that makes me happy.

============================================================
6. MODAL VERBS
============================================================

6A. CAN

1. These accounts (easily check) by an expert.
Answer: These accounts can be easily checked by an expert.

2. Mangoes (eat) while they are still green.
Answer: Mangoes can be eaten while they are still green.

3. Smoking (cause) lung cancer.
Answer: Smoking can cause lung cancer.

4. Photographs (not take) in this museum.
Answer: Photographs cannot be taken in this museum.

5. Security guards (leave) their posts for a toilet break during their work shift?
Answer: Can security guards leave their posts for a toilet break during their work shift?

6B. MUST / MAY

1. We (obey) the law of the country.
Answer: We must obey the law of the country.

2. Collin (select) for the competition as he is talented.
Answer: Collin may be selected for the competition as he is talented.

3. In the dry zone, canals (dig) to bring water from the rivers into the rice field.
Answer: In the dry zone, canals must be dug to bring water from the rivers into the rice field.

4. I (use) your phone please?
Answer: May I use your phone, please?

5. The new animated movie (show) next week.
Answer: The new animated movie may be shown next week.

6. Slippers (not wear) in the temple.
Answer: Slippers must not be worn in the temple.

7. Trees (not fall) and flowers (not pluck) in the park.
Answer: Trees must not be felled and flowers must not be plucked in the park.
Note: The PDF appears to use “fall”; “felled” is the natural verb if the meaning is “cut down.”

8. The project (finish) last night.
Answer: The project must have been finished last night.
Note: If the intended meaning is possibility, “The project may have been finished last night” is also possible.

9. You (come) if you wish.
Answer: You may come if you wish.

6C. SHOULD / COULD

1. Pork (cook) properly to kill worms.
Answer: Pork should be cooked properly to kill worms.

2. If he worked all night, he (finish) his work in time.
Answer: If he worked all night, he could finish his work in time.

3. The child (examine) by a doctor if she is unwell.
Answer: The child should be examined by a doctor if she is unwell.

4. I (go) to the party. If not, she will be upset.
Answer: I should go to the party. If not, she will be upset.

5. You please (turn) on the lights?
Answer: Could you please turn on the lights?

6D. COULD / WOULD

1. He said that he (travel) around the world if he were rich enough to do so.
Answer: He said that he would travel around the world if he were rich enough to do so.

2. He said he (finish) five plates of noodles at one sitting.
Answer: He said he could finish five plates of noodles at one sitting.

3. I (come) if I was invited.
Answer: I would come if I was invited.

4. You (tell) me when your professor will be back?
Answer: Could you tell me when your professor will be back?

5. Penelope (not stay) without sleeping in the afternoon.
Answer: Penelope could not stay without sleeping in the afternoon.

6. If you don’t feel well, you (not go) outside without an umbrella.
Answer: If you don’t feel well, you would not go outside without an umbrella.

============================================================
7. IT IS POSSIBLE THAT ... (MAY)
============================================================

1. It is possible that she will finish this job in twenty minutes.
Answer: She may finish this job in twenty minutes.

2. It is possible that a river flowing over a rock will dissolve some of its surface.
Answer: A river flowing over a rock may dissolve some of its surface.

3. It is possible that authors will write poems as well as novels or plays.
Answer: Authors may write poems as well as novels or plays.

4. It is possible that he telephoned while we are out.
Answer: He may have telephoned while we were out.

5. It is possible that the fire on the ship was started by a bomb.
Answer: The fire on the ship may have been started by a bomb.

6. If you eat less, it is possible that you will lose weight.
Answer: If you eat less, you may lose weight.

============================================================
8. OMITTING THE RELATIVE PRONOUNS
============================================================

1. She wore the jewellery that belonged to my mother.
Answer: She wore the jewellery belonging to my mother.

2. She went to look at the house which has recently been built by the construction company.
Answer: She went to look at the house recently built by the construction company.
Note: The PDF wording “look the house” and “by the construction” has been corrected to natural English.

3. I can’t remember the hotel that we stayed in.
Answer: I can’t remember the hotel we stayed in.

4. The foundation helps children who are facing difficulties to continue their education.
Answer: The foundation helps children facing difficulties to continue their education.

============================================================
9. TO + V1
============================================================

1. The poor woman worked hard. She wished to send her son to college.
Answer: The poor woman worked hard to send her son to college.

2. Trees are felled. They are used both for firewood and timber.
Answer: Trees are felled to be used both for firewood and timber.

3. I swim every day with the intention of being healthy.
Answer: I swim every day to be healthy.

4. Thiri studied hard. She passed the exam with flying colours.
Answer: Thiri studied hard to pass the exam with flying colours.

5. She is attending a secretary course. She wants to get a job in a company.
Answer: She is attending a secretary course to get a job in a company.

6. They are holding a meeting with the intention of proposing the new ideas for a new product.
Answer: They are holding a meeting to propose the new ideas for a new product.

============================================================
10. V-ING
============================================================

1. He broke the silence. He talked loudly.
Answer: Talking loudly, he broke the silence.

2. Prawns are preserved. They are dried and salted.
Answer: Prawns are preserved, being dried and salted.

3. Mother came back from the market. She carried a heavy basket.
Answer: Carrying a heavy basket, Mother came back from the market.

4. He visited several snares. He didn’t find anything to pick up.
Answer: Not finding anything to pick up, he visited several snares.

5. U Mya opened the door. He did not know who could come in.
Answer: Not knowing who could come in, U Mya opened the door.

6. Students listen to the lectures. They take down notes.
Answer: Listening to the lectures, students take down notes.

============================================================
11. WITHOUT + V-ING
============================================================

1. Some students sat for the IELTS exam. They did not take any practice tests.
Answer: Some students sat for the IELTS exam without taking any practice tests.

2. They have not been trained hard but they won their opponents.
Answer: They won against their opponents without being trained hard.

3. She entered the room. Nobody welcomed her.
Answer: She entered the room without being welcomed.

4. If you do not write your full address, you cannot post your letters.
Answer: Without writing your full address, you cannot post your letters.

5. The manager hired that woman as his personal assistant. He did not know anything about her.
Answer: The manager hired that woman as his personal assistant without knowing anything about her.

6. The little boy went to bed. He did not say his prayers.
Answer: The little boy went to bed without saying his prayers.

7. The village boy visited several snares. He did not find anything to pick up.
Answer: The village boy visited several snares without finding anything to pick up.

8. He repaired the tap. No one reminded him.
Answer: He repaired the tap without being reminded by anyone.

9. The presenter read out continuously. She did not take eye contact with the audience.
Answer: The presenter read out continuously without making eye contact with the audience.

============================================================
12. BY V-ING
============================================================

1. My students improve their writing. They are asked to write an essay every Friday.
Answer: My students improve their writing by being asked to write an essay every Friday.

2. Tea becomes strong. It is prepared for a long time.
Answer: Tea becomes strong by being prepared for a long time.

3. We can widen the scope of knowledge. We read journals and magazines.
Answer: We can widen the scope of knowledge by reading journals and magazines.

4. The patient became better. He took the medicine regularly.
Answer: The patient became better by taking the medicine regularly.

5. The duckling in the pond caught a small fish. It dived into the water.
Answer: The duckling in the pond caught a small fish by diving into the water.

6. My younger brother makes his parents happy. He obeys, cares, and supports them.
Answer: My younger brother makes his parents happy by obeying, caring for, and supporting them.

============================================================
13. EITHER ... OR / NEITHER ... NOR
============================================================

1. Dagon was not mentioned by visitors to Myanmar in the 14th century. Dagon was not mentioned by visitors to Myanmar in the 15th century.
Answer: Dagon was mentioned by visitors to Myanmar in neither the 14th nor the 15th century.

2. We have never seen the manager. We have never seen the director.
Answer: We have seen neither the manager nor the director.

3. You can accept my offer. You can reject my offer.
Answer: You can either accept or reject my offer.

4. A tropical storm can bring heavy rain and strong winds. It can bring a storm surge which results in flooding.
Answer: A tropical storm can bring either heavy rain and strong winds or a storm surge which results in flooding.

5. Neil isn’t interested in acting in movies. She isn’t interested in singing songs in front of the audience.
Answer: Neither is Neil interested in acting in movies, nor is she interested in singing songs in front of the audience.

6. In this game, you can win. In this game, you can lose.
Answer: In this game, you can either win or lose.

7. You return the money you had stolen. I call the police.
Answer: Either you return the money you had stolen or I call the police.

============================================================
14. NOT ONLY ... BUT ALSO
============================================================

1. A fax machine and a computer are important office tools.
Answer: Not only a fax machine but also a computer is an important office tool.

2. She showed her temper. She banged the doors to sleep well.
Answer: She not only showed her temper but also banged the doors to sleep well.

3. Composers create music to be played on different instruments. Composers create music to be sung.
Answer: Composers create not only music to be played on different instruments but also music to be sung.

4. The team went to the village headman’s house. The team went to the monastery, too.
Answer: The team went not only to the village headman’s house but also to the monastery.

5. Taylor Swift is a prominent cultural figure of the 21st century. Selena Gomez is a prominent cultural figure of the 21st century.
Answer: Not only Taylor Swift but also Selena Gomez is a prominent cultural figure of the 21st century.

6. Our family doctor takes care of grandpa’s health. He also takes care of grandma’s health.
Answer: Our family doctor not only takes care of grandpa’s health but also takes care of grandma’s health.

7. I like tea and so does my mother.
Answer: Not only do I like tea, but my mother also likes tea.

============================================================
15. SO ... (ADJ/ADV) ... THAT
============================================================

1. Germs are very tiny. We cannot see them with the naked eyes.
Answer: Germs are so tiny that we cannot see them with the naked eyes.

2. Few people attended the seminar. There were many vacant seats.
Answer: So few people attended the seminar that there were many vacant seats.

3. I had much work at the office. I hardly had time to scratch if I felt itchy.
Answer: I had so much work at the office that I hardly had time to scratch if I felt itchy.

4. The book is interesting. I don’t want to stop reading it.
Answer: The book is so interesting that I don’t want to stop reading it.

5. The night was very cold. We had to wear two coats.
Answer: The night was so cold that we had to wear two coats.

6. The researcher worked efficiently. He obtained the results very quickly.
Answer: The researcher worked so efficiently that he obtained the results very quickly.

7. The luggage was very heavy. No porter could carry it.
Answer: The luggage was so heavy that no porter could carry it.

16. ABOUT TO
============================================================

1. The passengers dived into the river when the boat (sink).
Answer: The passengers dived into the river when the boat was about to sink.

2. She (promote) if she gets a Ph.D.
Answer: She is about to be promoted if she gets a Ph.D.

3. I (raise) my hand in class when Leo shouted out the answer.
Answer: I was about to raise my hand in class when Leo shouted out the answer.

4. We (leave) the building when it started to rain.
Answer: We were about to leave the building when it started to rain.

5. He (sit) on the sofa when he saw a big snake coiling on it.
Answer: He was about to sit on the sofa when he saw a big snake coiling on it.

6. We are in time. The gate (close).
Answer: We are in time. The gate is about to close.

7. I (say) when you interrupted me.
Answer: I was about to say when you interrupted me.

============================================================
17. HAVE TO
============================================================

1. It is necessary for us to send text messages online instead of writing letters.
Answer: We have to send text messages online instead of writing letters.

2. It was necessary for him to buy a new house.
Answer: He had to buy a new house.

3. It will be necessary for me to forget him.
Answer: I will have to forget him.

4. It is necessary for all students to wear their ID card.
Answer: All students have to wear their ID card.

5. If you want to travel abroad, it will be necessary for you to have a passport.
Answer: If you want to travel abroad, you will have to have a passport.

6. It is necessary for us to queue all day to see the film.
Answer: We have to queue all day to see the film.

7. It is necessary for him to install up-to-date software in his laptop.
Answer: He has to install up-to-date software on his laptop.

18. BY MEANS OF
============================================================

1. Irrigation enables more than one crop to be cultivated in the same area within the same season.
Answer: By means of irrigation, more than one crop can be cultivated in the same area within the same season.

2. Storage reservoirs enable fish and wildlife to be protected.
Answer: By means of storage reservoirs, fish and wildlife can be protected.

3. In mountainous areas, multipurpose dams enable water to be held in storage reservoirs.
Answer: By means of multipurpose dams in mountainous areas, water can be held in storage reservoirs.

4. In flat, open areas, a system of canals enables irrigation to be carried out.
Answer: By means of a system of canals in flat, open areas, irrigation can be carried out.

5. Brakes enable a car to be stopped.
Answer: By means of brakes, a car can be stopped.

19. DIFFER IN + NOUN + FROM
============================================================

1. Monkeys and apes / intelligence / dogs and sea-lions.
Answer: Monkeys and apes differ in intelligence from dogs and sea-lions.

2. A horse / size / an elephant.
Answer: A horse differs in size from an elephant.

3. Cats / behavior / dogs.
Answer: Cats differ in behavior from dogs.

4. This material / texture / the other one.
Answer: This material differs in texture from the other one.

5. These cultures / language / each other.
Answer: These cultures differ in language from each other.

6. The iPhone / features and price / Samsung.
Answer: The iPhone differs in features and price from Samsung.

============================================================
20. AS IF + NOUN + VERB (PAST FORM)
============================================================

1. Mu Mu looked / be / not well.
Answer: Mu Mu looked as if she was not well.
Note: “Were” is also used in formal unreal-condition grammar, but the PDF’s own example uses “was.”

2. He behaves / be / the manager.
Answer: He behaves as if he were the manager.

3. She talks / knows / everything.
Answer: She talks as if she knew everything.

4. They treat me / I / do / something wrong.
Answer: They treat me as if I did something wrong.

5. He spends / money / be / a millionaire.
Answer: He spends money as if he were a millionaire.

6. The students used the term “mammals” / include all creatures that give birth to live young.
Answer: The students used the term “mammals” as if it included all creatures that give birth to live young.

7. The storm raged outside / be / a wild animal trying to break free.
Answer: The storm raged outside as if it were a wild animal trying to break free.
