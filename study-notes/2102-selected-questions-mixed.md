## လေ့ကျင့်ရန် မေးခွန်းများ

### 1. Unit II No. 6 — Power-series method

Solve the differential equation

`(x - 1)y' + 2y = 0`

by using the power-series method. Determine the radius of convergence of the resulting series.

### 2. Assignment 1 No. 4 — Initial-value problem

Solve the initial-value problem

`y''' + 3y'' - 10y' = 0`,

subject to

`y(0) = 7`, `y'(0) = 0`, `y''(0) = 70`.

### 3. Unit I No. 8 — Initial-value problem

Solve the initial-value problem

`y'' - 2y' + 2y = x + 1`,

subject to

`y(0) = 3`, `y'(0) = 0`.

### 4. Assignment 2 No. 4 — Power-series method

Find a power-series solution of the differential equation

`y'' + y = 0`.

> မှတ်ချက် — ဒီမေးခွန်းသည် Unit II No. 2 နှင့် တူညီသော မေးခွန်းဖြစ်သည်။

### 5. Unit I No. 4 — Given linearly independent solutions

The given `y_1 = e^x`, `y_2 = e^{2x}` and `y_3 = e^{3x}` are linearly independent solutions of the third-order homogeneous equation

`y''' - 6y'' + 11y' - 6y = 0`.

Find a particular solution of the given equation that satisfies the initial conditions

`y(0) = 0`, `y'(0) = 0`, `y''(0) = 3`.

### 6. Unit II No. 7 — Initial-value problem by power series

Solve the initial-value problem by using the power-series method:

`(x^2 + 1)y'' + 2xy' - 2y = 0`,

with

`y(0) = 0`, `y'(0) = 1`.

### 7. Assignment 1 No. 1 — Verification and repeated root

1. Verify that `y_1` and `y_2` are solutions of the differential equation

   `y'' - 2y' + 2y = 0`,

   where

   `y_1 = e^x cos x`, `y_2 = e^x sin x`.

2. Find the general solution of

   `9y'' - 12y' + 4y = 0`.

### 8. Unit I No. 9 — Variation of parameters

Find a particular solution of the differential equation

`y'' + 3y' + 2y = 4e^x`

by using the method of variation of parameters.

### 9. Unit II No. 5 — Power-series method and convergence

Solve the equation

`(x - 3)y' + 2y = 0`

by using the power-series method. Determine the radius of convergence of the resulting series.

### 10. Unit I No. 6 — Appropriate particular-solution form

Determine the appropriate form for a particular solution of

`y'' + 6y' + 13y = e^{-3x} cos 2x`.
